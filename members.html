Resident Apartment Guide

Essential operating instructions, building protocols, and resident amenities for your stay at Lily Terrace.

📶 High-Speed Wi-Fi

Network: LilyTerrace_Ultra

Password: ramongaya1956

Fiber optic connection covering all salon spaces and the upper terrace.

🔑 Key & Building Access

Front Street Door: Brass key (turn clockwise twice).

Palazzo Piano: Third piano (4th American floor). Handle three flights of stairs.

Apartment Door: High-security deadbolt key.

❄️ Climate & 6kW Power

Multi-zone heating and air conditioning controls throughout. The property is equipped with an upgraded 6kW electrical system.

🌿 Private Terrace Etiquette

Enjoy morning espresso and evening aperitivi on the rooftop terrace. Please retract the retractable awning during heavy winds or upon departure.

♻️ Waste & Recycling

Separated collection per Rome regulations:

  • Organic (Umido): Brown bin under sink
  • Paper & Cardboard: Blue bag
  • Plastic & Metal: Yellow bin
  • Glass: Green street bin (Piazza della Quercia)

🏛️ Heritage & Ramón Gaya

This residence was the creative sanctuary and home of legendary Spanish painter Ramón Gaya during his time in Rome. Please treat all historic materials and artwork with care.
