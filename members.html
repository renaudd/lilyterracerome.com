🔑 Keys & Building Access

Building Portal: Use the brass key to unlock the main green street door at Vicolo del Giglio 13.

Pad Entry: The apartment is located on the second piano (3rd floor). Use the upper deadbolt key followed by the handle lock.

Checking Out: Leave both sets of keys on the dining room table and pull the door firmly shut behind you.

❄️ Climate Control & A/C

Central A/C units are located in the living room, master bedroom, and kitchen. Remotes are mounted near each door.

To cool the sleeping nook, keep the living room A/C on and turn on the nook circulating fan.

The kitchen also features a ceiling fan with its own wall remote.

🧺 Laundry & Care

In-Unit Washer: Located in the main bathroom. Best suited for small loads. Hang dry on the veranda clotheslines.

Laundromat Across the Alley: Full-size express washers and dryers (30-minute dry cycles). Open by 9:00 AM daily. Accepts cash and credit cards.

🌿 Terrace / Patio Rules
  • Occupancy: Maximum of 4 persons at all times.
  • Quiet Hours: No amplified music or loud socializing after 10:00 PM out of respect for neighbors.
  • Awning: Extend the canvas awning to roughly above the railing during sunny afternoons; retract fully before heavy winds or rain.
🚿 Bathroom & Plumbing

The water heater operates on a dedicated 6kW supply ensuring strong pressure and rapid reheating.

Care Note: The knob controlling the overhead rainfall shower should be turned gently; pulling at an angle will detach the trim.
