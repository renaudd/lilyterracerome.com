Refined Dining

Contemporary Neapolitan Pizza
Piccolo Buco

Steps from the Trevi Fountain, this tiny gem delivers the city's most innovative pizza. Famous for its pillowy "canotto" crust and high-end toppings. Even if you show up before they open, expect to wait in line — it is worth it. Order the yellow spice salami pie.

Sandwich Shop & Lunch Plates · ★★★★ · $
Sto Bene

Closest sandwich shop to the apartment — very cheap, very good, and no wait. The Norcino (€8) is spectacular, and the orange juice (€3.50) is the cheapest around. Also does lunch plates. Popular with locals.

Neapolitan Snack Food Spot · ★★★★ · $$
Fiore

Neapolitan snack food spot — the montanare are awesome. Possibly the best supplì in town.

Florentine Sandwiches · ★★★★ · $$
All’Antico Vinaio

The one on Corso has the shortest line, but the quality control on Via delle Colonnelle is a little better. I recommend going at night right before they close to avoid the lines.

Pizza Pocket Sandwiches · ★★★★ · $$
Trapizzino

Triangular pizza pocket sandwiches. The closest one is right across from ponte sisto, but the one near the Vatican is attached to a great artisanal beer bar.

Neighborhood Hidden Gems · ★★★★★ · $
Ruma

Tell Gui I sent you. Possibly the best gelato, cheese, and wine you'll have in Europe. Reasonably priced. Good music. Great location. A modern masterclass in agricultural cuisine.

Roman Osteria & Pasta · ★★★★ · $$
La Quercia

If not just as good as Armando’s their Amatriciana is easily number two, and their menu overall has a lot of great stuff on it. A block from the apartment and generally no wait. Attire recommended but not mandatory.

Historic Local Café · ★★★ · $$
Caffè Perù

A classic neighborhood bar on Via di Monserrato. Popular with locals.

Crispy Roman Sandwiches · ★★★★ · $
Scrocchia

The Ambaradan tastes like a honey mustard snack wrap in the best way. The sandwiches are small but super cheap and top quality. Close, never a line.

Classic Roman Institution · ★★★★ · $$
Armando al Pantheon

Be on their website at 11:59 pm Italy time exactly one month before the day you want to eat there or you will not get to eat there. Definitely order the Amatriciana, it’s the best in town. The other stuff is just good, and the menu is heavy on offal.
